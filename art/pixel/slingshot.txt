# `bands` share one 18x23 grid: the frame is the body and the collider, and the bands — at
# rest, sagging to the pouch between the prongs — are hidden while the player draws, when the
# toy draws them itself from the prong tips to the pouch at the cursor (Slingshot._draw_bands).
#
#   prong tips   the band knots, at the grid corners (4, 2) and (14, 2)
#   fork         where a shot leaves: the middle of the gap, (9, 5)
#   grip         where the hand holds it and the joint pins: the crotch of the Y, (9, 12). The
#                weight is lower, in the handle, so it hangs fork-up from the hand.
#
# `pouch` is the leather the player pulls, and `pellet` what it throws, each on a small grid
# of its own and placed by the toy. `outline: off`, so the bands stay one pixel wide; every
# outline here is drawn by hand.
