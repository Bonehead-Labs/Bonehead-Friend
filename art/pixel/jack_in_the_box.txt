# Jack-in-the-box (D57). `main` is the toy as the shop and the icon show it — sprung, lid
# thrown back — on its own grid. The four moving parts share one 36x24 grid so they line up
# in the cell with no offsets, and the box is dead centre of it (cols 7-28, rows 3-20), which
# is what lets its collider be derived from its own pixels (D55):
#
#   box    the body; the collider and the glow are this
#   lid    rows 0-2, hinged at its bottom-left corner — (6, 3) on the grid — and swung open
#   crank  on the right side, turning about its axle at the grid corner (31, 13)
#   jack   the clown and his spring, drawn *inside* the box, behind it; the pop lifts him
#          17 art px, which puts his head clear of the lid and leaves his spring in the box
#
# The whole assembly mirrors when the box is turned round (JackInTheBox.set_facing): the crank
# stays on the side away from the hinge, and the zones follow (GestureZones.mirrored).
id: jack_in_the_box
