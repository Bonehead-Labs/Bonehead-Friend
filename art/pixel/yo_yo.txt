# Yo-yo (D66). Face on, because that is how it is seen spinning at the end of its string: a
# red disc with a dark rim, a gold axle cap and a white glint. The glint and the dark lower-
# right rim are what make a spin visible — a flat red disc turning looks exactly like one
# standing still. The disc is 14x14, a fifth of his height.
#
# Three parts on one 34x34 grid, the disc dead centre of it so the body's origin is the axle:
#
#   disc   the body and the collider (a circle, authored in seed_m39_toys2)
#   tail   the loose end of the string with its finger loop, shown while the yo-yo is in the
#          hand or on the desk. It is what says "yo-yo" rather than "red ball" at rest.
#   main   both, for the shop
#
# `outline: off`, so the string stays one pixel wide. Out on its string the toy draws the
# string itself, from the hand to the axle (YoYo._draw).
id: yo_yo
cell: 64
outline: off
