# Slinky (D66). A rainbow plastic one, because a steel one is a grey smudge at 1x on a dark
# desk and the colours are what make it read as coils rather than as a tin can. `main` is the
# toy at rest — seven coils stacked, the top one open so the hole says "spring" — and is the
# body, the collider and the shop picture.
#
# `ring0`..`ring5` are one coil each, seen edge-on, one per colour in the stack's own order.
# Stretched, the toy hides `main` and lays these along the curve between its two ends
# (Slinky._lay_coil), cycling the colours, so a pulled slinky is the same six colours spread
# out. Each ring is 16 wide like the stack, so a coil does not change width as it opens.
