BONEHEAD FRIEND - playtest build {{BUILD_ID}}
====================================================

Thank you for trying it. It is an unfinished game and you are among the first people to
play it. Everything you run into is useful to us - the confusing parts most of all.


RUNNING IT
----------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (the Desktop is fine). Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "Bonehead Friend.exe".
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the game is not signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. A skeleton appears on top of your desktop. That is the game. It stays above your
   other windows while you work - that is on purpose.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
- There is no tutorial beyond a couple of hints. Try things; there is no wrong way to play.
- It keeps going while you do something else. Leave it running while you work if you like.
- To close it: press Esc, then "Save and quit". Your progress is kept.
- The menus are at the top right. Settings has the window size, sound and Focus Mode.
- If it covers too much of your screen: Settings > Window > Play area makes it a smaller
  window, and the little grip at the top centre moves it anywhere, even to another monitor.


TELLING US WHAT YOU THINK
-------------------------
- Press F1 at any moment to leave a note: how it is going (Good / Meh / Bad), what
  happened, and what you were trying to do. Short is fine. Leave as many as you like.
  The same button is on the Esc menu and at the bottom of Settings.
{{SENDING}}


WHAT THE GAME RECORDS (AND WHAT IT DOES NOT)
--------------------------------------------
This build keeps a small log of play on your computer: when you start and stop, what you
buy, which toys and abilities you try, which menus you open, how long between clicks, and
any error messages the game runs into. Each note you save also keeps a picture of the
game's own window at that moment (never your desktop or other windows).

It records no name, no files, nothing you type anywhere except into a note, and nothing
from other programs. Nothing leaves your computer until you send it.

You can switch the log off in Settings > Playtest > Session log, and look at everything
it has written with Settings > Playtest > Open folder. The files live in:
    {{DATA_DIR}}\playtest


REMOVING IT
-----------
Delete this folder. To remove the save and the logs as well, delete:
    {{DATA_DIR}}
